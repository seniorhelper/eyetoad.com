How to Choose a Denver SEO Company: 9 Questions to Ask Before You Sign

By Eye To Ad MediaJune 20269 min read

Picking the wrong agency doesn't just waste money — it can quietly cost you a full year of growth while a competitor pulls ahead. Denver is one of the most competitive local search markets in Colorado, and the gap between a great SEO partner and a bad one is enormous.

The hard part is that almost every agency sounds the same on a sales call. Everyone promises rankings, traffic, and leads. So instead of grading the pitch, grade the answers. Below are the nine questions that reliably separate a real Denver SEO company from one that's selling you a dashboard and a prayer. Ask all nine before you sign anything.

Quick Answer

To choose a good Denver SEO company, ask how they report results, what their realistic timeline is, whether they optimize for AI search and Google Maps, who actually does the work, and whether they'll show you local references. Avoid anyone guaranteeing fast page-one rankings or refusing to explain their process.

Question 01

"Can you show me results from other Denver-area clients?"

National case studies are nice, but Denver is its own animal. A company that genuinely understands this market should be able to point to real local wins — map pack rankings, organic growth, or lead increases for Colorado businesses. Vague screenshots without context are a warning sign. Real proof is specific.

Question 02

"What's a realistic timeline for my market?"

Anyone promising page one in two weeks is either misinformed or lying. In a competitive term like Denver SEO, meaningful movement usually takes three to six months, with the strongest compounding results showing up after six to twelve. An honest agency sets expectations instead of selling miracles.

Question 03

"How do you report results — and what do you actually track?"

Rankings alone don't pay the bills. You want a partner who reports on calls, form fills, and qualified leads, not just a position-tracking chart. If reporting is fuzzy or they dodge the question, assume the results will be fuzzy too.

Question 04

"Do you optimize for AI search, not just Google?"

This is the question that exposes whether an agency is current. A growing share of Denver searches now surface Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, and Perplexity answers before traditional listings. A modern Denver SEO company should have a clear answer for how they earn visibility inside those AI results.

Question 05

"Who actually does the work?"

Plenty of agencies sell the deal locally and then ship your account overseas to a content mill. Ask who writes your content, who builds your links, and whether you'll have a real point of contact. The answer tells you a lot about the quality you'll get.

Question 06

"What's your approach to Google Maps and the local pack?"

For most Denver businesses, the map pack drives the phone calls. A capable team should talk fluently about Google Business Profile optimization, review strategy, local citations, and proximity. If they only talk about your website, they're missing half the opportunity.

Question 07

"Are you building real links — or buying spam?"

Cheap link packages can tank a site that was finally gaining traction. Ask how they earn links and whether they follow Google's guidelines. A trustworthy partner builds authority slowly and safely rather than risking a penalty for a quick bump.

Question 08

"What happens if I want to leave?"

Read the contract. Who owns the content, the website, and the work product if the relationship ends? An agency confident in its results doesn't need to trap you with long lock-ins and hostage clauses. Ownership should be yours.

Question 09

"Will you fix my conversion path, or just send traffic?"

Traffic to a broken website is wasted spend. The best partners care whether visitors actually become customers — page speed, clear calls to action, working forms. If they never mention conversion, they're optimizing for vanity, not revenue.

The bottom line

A great Denver SEO company will welcome every one of these questions — because clear answers are exactly what sets them apart. If an agency gets defensive, talks in circles, or leans on guarantees that sound too good to be true, trust that instinct and keep looking.

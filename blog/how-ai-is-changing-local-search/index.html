How AI Is Changing Local Search — What Denver Businesses Need to Know

The way people find local businesses is being restructured by AI. Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, and Perplexity are all routing local queries differently than traditional search. Here's what's changing and what to do about it.

If you've been paying attention to search over the past year, you've noticed something different. The results page looks different. The way answers appear has changed. And increasingly, people are skipping Google entirely and asking ChatGPT to find them a local contractor, dentist, or HVAC company. This isn't a niche trend — it's a fundamental restructuring of how local customers find businesses, and it's happening fast.

For Denver business owners, understanding what's changing — and why — is the first step toward staying visible through the transition. Here's a clear-eyed look at what AI is doing to local search and what you can do about it.

Google AI Overviews: The Map Pack Has a New Neighbor

Google's AI Overviews now appear above the map pack and organic results for a growing percentage of local service queries. These are AI-generated summaries that synthesize information from multiple sources and present an answer — often naming specific businesses — before any traditional results appear.

When an AI Overview appears for a query your business should be winning, and your business isn't named in it, you've lost visibility you used to own — even if you still rank #1 organically below it. The clicks that once went to your page-one listing increasingly go to the AI Overview and the sources it cites.

The businesses appearing in AI Overviews tend to share common characteristics: strong E-E-A-T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness), FAQ-structured content that directly answers common questions, high review volume and quality, and domain authority built over time. These aren't new SEO concepts — they're the same fundamentals that have always mattered, now more important than ever.

ChatGPT Search: A New Front Door for Local Customers

ChatGPT's web search capability has created a new channel through which local customers discover businesses. When a user asks ChatGPT "Who's the best roofing company in Denver?" or "Find me a good dentist near Lakewood," ChatGPT searches the web, synthesizes what it finds, and makes specific recommendations — often with explanations of why it's recommending each business.

The businesses ChatGPT tends to recommend are those with strong, consistent online presences: well-optimized websites with clear service descriptions, robust Google review profiles, presence on major directories like Yelp and BBB, and any editorial coverage from local news or industry publications. Essentially, businesses that have done thorough traditional local SEO have a head start in AI search visibility.

Test this yourself: Open ChatGPT and ask it to recommend a [your service type] in Denver. See who appears. If competitors are showing up and you're not, examining their online presence will tell you exactly what signals ChatGPT is drawing from.

Perplexity: The AI Search Engine for High-Value Customers

Perplexity has grown into a significant search engine with a user base that skews toward educated, higher-income, and tech-forward searchers — exactly the demographic many Denver service businesses most want to reach. Perplexity's answers are heavily citation-based, drawing from authoritative web sources, review platforms, and business directories.

Optimization for Perplexity largely overlaps with optimization for Google and ChatGPT: strong website content, robust reviews, consistent directory presence, and any earned media or editorial coverage. The signal set is similar; the output format differs.

What Hasn't Changed

Despite all this disruption, the core signals that drive local visibility remain consistent across all platforms: credibility, authority, relevance, and genuine positive reputation. The businesses succeeding in AI search are largely the same businesses that have been doing traditional local SEO correctly. AI search rewards the same fundamentals — it just surfaces them in new ways.

What has changed is the urgency of getting these fundamentals right. When traditional search was the only channel, a business could survive with a mediocre online presence if competition was limited. AI search surfaces the best-credentialed businesses across multiple platforms simultaneously, making it harder for the second-best option to win on any of them.

What Denver Businesses Should Do Right Now

  1. Audit your E-E-A-T signals. Does your website clearly demonstrate real expertise and experience? Are there genuine credentials, real staff photos, and substantive content that demonstrates knowledge of your field?
  2. Build FAQ content. AI search engines pull directly from question-and-answer content. Every service page should have a robust FAQ section addressing the questions customers actually ask.
  3. Accelerate review generation. Reviews are cited across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity. Consistent fresh reviews on Google and Yelp improve visibility on all AI platforms simultaneously.
  4. Strengthen directory presence. Ensure your business is accurately listed on all major directories — Google, Yelp, BBB, Bing Places, Apple Maps, and industry-specific platforms relevant to your business.
  5. Pursue local editorial coverage. A mention in a Denver local publication, a guest article in an industry blog, or a quote in a local news piece all create the kind of authoritative references that AI search engines weigh heavily.

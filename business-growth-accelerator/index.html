Why groups, and why now

Local groups are where people actually ask for recommendations.

Before someone searches, they ask. "Anyone know a good roofer?" gets forty replies in a local group in an hour, and the businesses named there get the call — no ranking required. We didn't rent access to those rooms; we built and administer them, and we protect them, because a group full of spam is a group nobody reads.

That's why this is a membership. Members get seen in communities that trust the admin, with a plan behind the post and a page behind the click. Then the search side — Maps, SEO, AI visibility — catches the people who go looking afterward. How it all fits together →

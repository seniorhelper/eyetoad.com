How does working with our agency go, stage by stage?

Every engagement moves through four stages: a free audit, a foundation stage that fixes what is broken, a build stage that adds the pages and profile work your market needs, and a compounding stage where results are measured in calls and the plan is adjusted.

  1. The audit

    Free, and yours to keep. We check speed, rankings, your map listing, what AI assistants say about your category and, above all, whether the site converts. Start at the free SEO audit page.

  2. Foundation

    Technical fixes, tracking that counts phone calls, Google Business Profile cleanup and the pages that are closest to ranking. Nothing fancy. Just the leaks, closed.

  3. Build

    New pages for searches you should own, review generation, local signals across the metro and AI search visibility so assistants can find and quote you.

  4. Compounding

    Monthly work guided by lead data. What produces calls gets more budget. What does not gets cut, and we tell you which is which.

Timelines vary by market and starting point. Our plain-English guide to the first 90 days shows what is realistic.

You talk to the people doing the work, starting with the founder. We are a small, founder-led agency by design. There is no sales team that hands you to an account manager who hands you to a junior. The person who explains the plan is accountable for the plan.

Terms are set per engagement and we keep them short. We would rather earn the next month than trap you in it. The free audit carries no contract and no obligation at all.

In everyday use, none. People search both phrases for the same thing. If you are comparing providers, our seven-test guide on how to choose a Denver SEO company applies to any of them, whatever they call themselves.

By the numbers that matter to a business: calls, form leads and booked jobs from search, tracked from day one. Rankings and traffic are reported too, but we treat them as means. If the phone is not ringing more, we say so.

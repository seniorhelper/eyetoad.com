Southern Utah search through the year

Summer heat, fall events and winter residents reshape St. George demand.

Google weighs relevance, distance and prominence when it builds local results, and in Washington County distance matters more than most owners expect. The towns are separated by open desert and I-15 interchanges, so a profile near downtown St. George seldom ranks for searches in Hurricane or Ivins without pages and reviews from those places.

Summer: heat and monsoon

Afternoons regularly top 100°F in July, which drives AC repair, pool service, window tinting and pest control. Late-summer monsoon storms can cause flash flooding in washes and streets, bringing drainage, roofing and restoration searches.

Fall: races and games

The St. George Marathon and the Huntsman World Senior Games both land in October, filling hotels and restaurants and lifting physical therapy, sports medicine and rental searches across town.

Winter and spring: snowbirds and Zion

Mild winters bring seasonal residents for golf and pickleball, and Zion National Park counted roughly 4.9 million visits in 2024. Hurricane, Springdale and LaVerkin businesses tied to tourism see spring and fall peaks.

The year-round base is broad: Intermountain Health’s St. George Regional Hospital and its specialty clinics, Utah Tech University, construction trades serving new subdivisions, and a large retiree population — roughly one in five residents is 65 or older. Each of these groups searches differently, which is why the plan focuses on the two or three that match your business.

What it means for marketing in St. George

  • St. George grew from 95,353 residents in 2020 to an estimated 106,288 in 2024, about 11% in four years. Every new household is shopping for a dentist, a plumber and a mechanic with no loyalty yet, so being visible in Maps when they first search matters more here than in a settled city.
  • Only 11% of St. George homes were built in 1979 or earlier, versus 36.6% across Utah and 50.5% nationally. Repair-heavy trades will find less aging housing stock than in most cities; new-build services such as landscaping, window tinting, pools, solar and finish work fit the market better.
  • The median age is 37.9, six years older than Utah's 31.7, and median household income is $72,870 against a state figure of $91,750. Clear pricing, reviews and an easy phone number usually do more for this audience than flashy pages.

Southern Utah search through the year

Summer heat, fall events and winter residents reshape St. George demand.

Google weighs relevance, distance and prominence when it builds local results, and in Washington County distance matters more than most owners expect. The towns are separated by open desert and I-15 interchanges, so a profile near downtown St. George seldom ranks for searches in Hurricane or Ivins without pages and reviews from those places.

Summer: heat and monsoon

Afternoons regularly top 100°F in July, which drives AC repair, pool service, window tinting and pest control. Late-summer monsoon storms can cause flash flooding in washes and streets, bringing drainage, roofing and restoration searches.

Fall: races and games

The St. George Marathon and the Huntsman World Senior Games both land in October, filling hotels and restaurants and lifting physical therapy, sports medicine and rental searches across town.

Winter and spring: snowbirds and Zion

Mild winters bring seasonal residents for golf and pickleball, and Zion National Park, about an hour away, draws visitors in the millions every year. Hurricane, Springdale and LaVerkin businesses tied to tourism see spring and fall peaks.

The year-round base is broad: Intermountain Health’s St. George Regional Hospital and its specialty clinics, Utah Tech University, construction trades serving new subdivisions, and a large retiree population that helps keep the city's median age at 37.9, against 31.7 for Utah as a whole. Each of these groups searches differently, which is why the plan focuses on the two or three that match your business.

Why Tampa is its own market

Water decides who drives where. Plan around the bridges.

The Howard Frankland, the Gandy and the Courtney Campbell are the real borders of Tampa Bay search. A roofer in Brandon isn't competing in Clearwater; a dentist in St. Pete isn't competing in Wesley Chapel. Google Maps has learned the geography. The plan has to as well.

The bay is three markets

Tampa (South Tampa, Westchase, Carrollwood, New Tampa), St. Petersburg, Clearwater/Largo — plus Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel and Lakeland feeding in. Each gets its own page and its own service-area signal.

Seasonal residents change the math

Winter brings residents who need a dentist, a handyman, a clinic — and who search for one from scratch. Content and profiles ready by October catch a demand curve a static site never sees.

Why is Colorado SEO not one campaign?

Colorado SEO is search marketing built for how Coloradans actually search: by city and county, with a Front Range that behaves like one connected metro and mountain and Western Slope markets that behave like separate states. A statewide campaign is really a set of local campaigns run under one plan.

The Front Range corridor from Fort Collins to Pueblo holds most of the population and most of the competition. Businesses there compete with Denver agencies and national brands. Mountain towns and the Western Slope have thinner search volume, far less competition and customers who decide by trust and word of mouth.

We build for both. Start with the market pages: Denver, Boulder, Colorado Springs, Fort Collins and Longmont, or see every service area.

What does a Colorado SEO campaign include?

Market-by-market keyword targets, one real page per city you serve, Google Business Profile work per location, reviews that name the town, and AI search visibility. Reporting is by market, so you see where the calls come from.

Questions, answered straight

Do you serve all of Colorado?

Yes. Our office is in Denver and we run campaigns statewide by phone and video, in person along the Front Range when it helps.

Is Colorado SEO different from Denver SEO?

Denver SEO targets one metro. Colorado SEO plans across markets that differ sharply in competition and volume, and it is usually the right frame for a business with more than one location or a statewide service area.

How much does statewide SEO cost?

Most multi-market campaigns run $1,000 to $3,000 a month depending on the number of markets. Single-market work runs $500 to $3,000. There is a $100 entry option.

Should I build a page for every Colorado town I serve?

No. Build pages for towns with real demand and real material, and group the rest by county. Thin swapped pages hurt the whole site.

Do you also handle Colorado Springs and Fort Collins?

Yes, each has its own page and its own strategy. Our founder spent five years in Fort Collins and grew up in Boulder.

Why Auckland is its own market

The harbour splits the map. Plan each side.

Auckland’s geography makes it a set of separate local markets. A tradie on the North Shore is not competing in Manukau, and a clinic in Botany is not competing in Ponsonby.

Bridges and motorways set the boundaries

The harbour bridge and the motorways shape where people will travel for a service. Each area needs its own page and service-area signals.

A diverse city searches in many languages

Auckland has large Pāsifika and Asian communities. Honest coverage in the languages your customers use, not machine translation, wins searches an English-only site never sees.

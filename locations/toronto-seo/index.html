Why Toronto is its own market

The 416 and the 905 are different markets. Plan both.

The GTA is one of the most competitive local-search markets in North America, and one of the most fragmented: a plumber in Brampton isn't competing downtown, and a dentist in Markham isn't competing in Etobicoke. National agencies build one Toronto page and wonder why the suburbs never call.

The 905 is its own economy

Mississauga, Brampton, Markham, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Oakville, Pickering. Family and immigrant-business markets with heavy home-services and healthcare demand, each with its own map pack and its own page in a plan that works.

Multilingual search is normal here

A meaningful share of GTA customers search in Mandarin, Cantonese, Punjabi, Tamil, Portuguese, Italian or Spanish. Honest coverage where it fits — not machine-translated pages — wins searches a single English site never sees.

Winter and condo density shape demand

Furnace, snow and ice searches in January; condo-dense downtown neighborhoods that search on foot; suburbs that search by car. The plan has to be specific about where and when.

Why is the schedule full but the margin thin?

Most dermatology practices are booked weeks out on medical visits that reimburse at contracted rates, while the elective procedures that pay at retail fill whatever is left. Marketing that only says “book an appointment” feeds the crowded side. Marketing built around the procedures you want more of changes the mix without adding hours.

The practices winning elective work are not the ones with the most impressive credentials page. They are the ones a patient finds when she searches a specific procedure in her city, reads a plain explanation of cost and recovery, and books without a phone call.

You are going to pay for a website either way. The only real question is whether it is built to bring you customers, or built to sit there looking fine.Zachary Wennstedt, founder

Dermatology practice or med spa?

They compete for the same cosmetic searches and are different businesses. A med spa lives on elective demand and repeat visits, and has its own med spa marketing page here. If your practice runs both under one roof, the split matters even more, and we will map it before writing a page.

Dermatology marketing questions, answered straight

What is dermatology marketing?

Dermatology marketing is the work of filling a practice with the right mix of patients: medical visits that keep the schedule full and elective visits that keep it profitable. It covers search visibility for conditions and procedures, Google Maps, reviews, and a booking path that separates the two kinds of patient.

Why does my full dermatology schedule not feel profitable?

Because a schedule full of short medical visits reimburses modestly, while elective work such as cosmetic procedures pays at retail and is the first thing to slip when the medical calendar is packed. The simulator on this page shows what a small shift toward elective does to the same number of slots.

How much does SEO cost for a dermatology practice?

Most of our dermatology campaigns run $500 to $3,000 a month depending on locations and the procedures you want to grow, with a $100 entry option. Published surveys put the average agency retainer a little above $3,000 a month.

Can you guarantee we rank for a procedure in our city?

No, and no honest agency can. Google states that nobody can guarantee a ranking. We promise the work, honest reporting tied to consultations booked, and a straight answer when a budget cannot win a market.

Is this the same as med spa marketing?

No. Med spas live on elective demand and repeat visits. A dermatology practice usually has the opposite problem, booked out on medical work and short on elective. The pages, offers and search terms differ, so we keep a separate med spa marketing page.

Do you handle medical advertising rules?

Yes. Outcome claims stay conservative, before-and-after content carries required context, credentials are stated accurately, and you approve every claim before it goes live.

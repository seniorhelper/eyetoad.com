Common Questions

Dermatology & Plastic Surgery SEO Denver — FAQs

Why does dermatology and plastic surgery SEO need to be different?
Health and aesthetic content is held to a higher standard by Google (E-E-A-T) and by patients making expensive, personal decisions. Generic SEO won't cut it — you need medically credible, deeply-built procedure content with real provider authority and proof. That's exactly what we build.
How long does aesthetic SEO take?
Because patients research over weeks and competition is meaningful, expect early movement in 3 to 6 months and strong procedure rankings over 6 to 12 months. The high lifetime value of aesthetic patients makes the investment pay back quickly once it lands.
Can you handle both cosmetic and medical dermatology?
Yes. They attract different patients and search differently — cosmetic is appearance and research driven, medical is condition and urgency driven. We build distinct content tracks for each so you capture both audiences.
Will this help me appear in AI search?
Yes. When patients ask ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews about a procedure or for a Denver provider, we build the structured data and authority that surface your practice. Health-AI visibility is a major emerging channel few practices have addressed.
What procedures should a Denver dermatology or plastic surgery practice build SEO pages for?
The highest-value procedure pages typically span both tracks: cosmetic procedures like Botox, dermal fillers, laser skin resurfacing, rhinoplasty, facelift, and body contouring; and medical procedures like Mohs micrographic surgery, skin cancer screening, acne treatment, and eczema or psoriasis care. Each deserves its own dedicated, in-depth page because patients search for them with distinct intent — see the procedure priority map above for how each one is searched.

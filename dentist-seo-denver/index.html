Dental Patients in Denver Start Their Search Online — and Choose From the Top 3

The vast majority of new dental patients begin with a Google search. Most never scroll past the first three results in the local map pack. That means if your practice isn't in the top 3 for the searches your patients are making — whether that's "dentist Aurora CO," "cosmetic dentist near me," or "same day dental appointment Denver" — those patients are calling your competitors instead.

Dental SEO is also different from general SEO in a few important ways. Dental searches are highly service-specific — a patient searching for dental implants has very different intent than one searching for a pediatric dentist. Each service needs its own targeted page, its own keyword strategy, and its own conversion path.

Trust is also uniquely important in dental. Patients are choosing someone to work inside their mouth. Reviews, credentials, real photos of your team and office, and clear before/after case studies all play a significant role in whether a visitor picks up the phone or clicks back to find someone else.

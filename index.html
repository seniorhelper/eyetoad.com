Common Questions

Denver SEO Questions We Hear Every Day

Honest answers to the questions Denver business owners ask us before getting started.

A Denver SEO company researches the keywords your ideal customers are typing into Google, then optimizes your website's technical health, content, and authority to rank for those terms. Beyond rankings, we also optimize for Google Maps visibility and — increasingly — AI-generated search results. The end goal is more high-quality visitors finding your business first.

Most businesses see meaningful ranking movement within 3 to 6 months. Highly competitive keywords in saturated markets can take 6 to 12 months. Local SEO in Google Maps often moves faster — sometimes within 30 to 60 days. The timeline depends on your current site authority, the competition level, and how aggressively we build content and links.

AIO stands for AI Optimization. Traditional SEO targets Google's blue links. AIO SEO means your content is also built to appear in Google's AI Overviews, ChatGPT answers, and Perplexity responses. As more people use AI to find businesses and services, being visible in those results requires structuring content differently than classic SEO alone. We build both into every strategy.

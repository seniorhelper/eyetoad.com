Why do homeowners call the plumber they find first?

Because a burst pipe does not leave time to compare. Emergency plumbing searches convert on the first credible result: the map listing with recent reviews, a real phone number and hours that say open now. If that is not you, the job went to whoever was easier to find, not whoever was better.

Our founder ran sales for plumbing companies doing millions a year. The pattern never changed: the companies with full boards were not the best plumbers in town. They were the ones a stressed homeowner found in ninety seconds on a phone.

They’re not better than you. They’re just easier to find.Zachary Wennstedt, founder

What does plumbing SEO include?

A plumbing SEO campaign covers four things: the Google Business Profile and map pack, a page for every service that gets searched (water heater replacement, drain cleaning, sewer line repair, repiping), reviews that mention the job and the town, and call tracking so you know which searches produce revenue.

Map pack first

For emergency and repair searches the map pack takes most of the calls. Categories, service lists, photos of real trucks and a steady flow of reviews. Our local SEO and Google Maps service is built around it.

One page per job type

“Tankless water heater install” and “sewer camera inspection” are different customers with different urgency. A page for each, written the way a homeowner searches, beats one services page every time.

AI answers

People now ask assistants who to call. Pages with plain facts, prices where possible and clear service areas get quoted. That is our AI SEO service, and our case study shows what it looks like in a home-services market.

Calls, not clicks

A plumbing site that ranks and does not convert is an expense. Sticky call buttons, a form that takes twenty seconds and proof where doubt shows up: conversion work is part of the job.

What about lead platforms and paid ads?

Shared-lead platforms sell the same homeowner to several plumbers at once. You pay whether you win the job or not, and the price is set by the platform, not you. Ads work for high-ticket jobs when the landing page converts, and they stop the moment the budget does.

SEO is the asset you own. It takes longer to build and it keeps producing after the invoice stops. Most plumbers run paid channels while the owned asset grows, then cut them back. For the wider playbook on contractor lead generation, Advertising For Contractors covers the whole trades picture.

Plumbing SEO questions, answered straight

What is plumbing SEO?

Plumbing SEO is the work of getting a plumbing company found by people searching for a plumber in its service area: in Google results, in the Google Maps pack and in AI answers. It covers the Google Business Profile, service pages for each job type, reviews, technical health and the conversion path from a search to a booked call.

How much does SEO cost for a plumbing company?

Industry surveys put the average agency retainer a little over $3,000 a month. Most of our plumbing campaigns run $500 to $3,000 a month depending on trucks, service area and competition, with a $100 entry option. We publish the reasoning on our SEO pricing page.

How long before a plumber sees results from SEO?

Map pack movement often shows within a few months when the Google Business Profile has been neglected, because there is easy ground to make up. Organic rankings for service pages build over six to twelve months. Nobody can promise a date, and Google says so itself.

Should a plumber do SEO or pay for leads?

Both can work, but they are different purchases. Shared-lead platforms sell the same homeowner to several plumbers at once, and the price rises when they need to. SEO builds an asset you own. Many of our clients run both while the owned asset grows, then cut the lead spend.

Do you understand the plumbing business or just marketing?

Our founder spent years as a sales manager for multi-million-dollar plumbing companies before he ever sold marketing. He knows what a slow Tuesday costs and what a tech does when the board is empty. That is where our plumbing pages come from.

Do you only work with Denver plumbers?

No. Denver is home, and we work with plumbing companies nationally. Every market gets its own plan, because the searches, the competitors and the seasons are different in Phoenix, Omaha and Denver.

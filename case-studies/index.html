What does this results page actually prove?

It proves one company can hold several positions for a competitive local keyword at the same time. On September 21, 2026, a Denver search for “denver walk in tubs” showed Aging Safely Baths first in the map pack, its funnel site first in organic results, and its main site again lower on the page.

Walk-in tubs are not a sleepy market. The same results page carried four paid ads at the top and more further down, from national manufacturers with real advertising budgets. Those companies pay for every click. The three red boxes above cost nothing per click.

There was also a fourth mention. The Better Business Bureau’s own category page ranked on that results page, and its preview text named Aging Safely first. We did not count it, because it is the BBB’s listing and not ours. It still puts the name in front of the searcher one more time.

A website that isn’t found is worthless. A website that doesn’t convert is a waste of your time and money.Zachary Wennstedt, founder

How do you end up with three listings for one keyword?

You stop treating “the website” as the only asset. Google fills a local results page from different sources: the map pack draws on Google Business Profiles, and organic results draw on web pages. A company that builds a strong entry for each source can appear more than once.

Door one: the map listing

The Google Business Profile for Aging Safely Walk In Bathtubs carries real reviews, a real address, accurate categories and long business history. The map pack is its own competition with its own rules. See how we approach it on our Denver local SEO and Google Maps page.

Door two: the focused site

denverwalkintub.com does one job. It talks about walk-in tubs in Denver and nothing else, and it is built to turn a visitor into a phone call. A page with one purpose is easier for Google to understand and easier for a visitor to act on. That second half is conversion work, not ranking work.

Door three: the brand site

agingsafelybaths.com is the full company site, with a location page written for Colorado. It earns its position through depth: product pages, service areas and years of published material about one subject.

None of this is a trick. Each asset is a real, useful thing a customer can use. That is also why it has lasted. Shortcuts get found and removed. Useful pages tend to stay.

Why show our own company and not a client?

Because it is the one campaign where we can tell you everything. Client numbers belong to clients. This one belongs to our founder, so nothing here is borrowed, rounded up or shown with permission that could be withdrawn.

It also answers the fairest question you can ask a marketing company: do you use this yourself? Yes. When the phone is quiet at Aging Safely Baths, it is our founder’s own income on the line. The methods we recommend to you are the ones that business depends on.

We keep client work on our main case studies page, with the limits of each number spelled out, and the sites we have built are on the Our Work page.

Questions people ask about this case study

Is Aging Safely Baths a client of Eye To Ad Media?

No, and that is the point. Aging Safely Baths is owned by our founder, Zachary Wennstedt. He built its search presence with the same methods we use for clients, using his own money and answering his own phones. We disclose that because a case study about your own company only counts if you say so.

Will I see the same results if I search it myself?

You may not see the identical page. Google results change by location, device, search history and day. The layout shown here is a recreation of what an incognito search from Denver returned on September 21, 2026. We invite you to run the search yourself, and we would rather you check than take our word for it.

Can you get my business three listings on one results page?

We cannot promise that, and nobody honestly can. Google’s own guidance says no one can guarantee a ranking. What we can do is build the same three kinds of assets for you: a strong Google Business Profile, a main site with a real local page, and, where it makes sense, a focused single-purpose site. How many of them rank is up to Google and your market.

Why does owning more than one listing matter?

Because a searcher who skips one result often clicks the next one. When two or three of the visible options lead to the same company, the odds that the call comes to you go up, and the space available to competitors goes down.

How long did this take?

Years, not weeks. Aging Safely Baths has been operating and publishing for well over a decade, and the map listing shows 15+ years in business. Anyone who tells you this kind of coverage appears in 90 days is selling something.

Agentic optimization

The next customer might be an agent.

AI agents now research, shortlist and increasingly book on a person's behalf. An agent doesn't scroll, doesn't admire a hero image and doesn't call to ask. It reads what's there, verifies it against other sources, and picks. Agentic optimization means being the business an agent can act on.

Readable

Services, service area, pricing signals, hours and contact paths stated in plain text and structured data — not buried in an image or a PDF.

Verifiable

The same facts confirmed by your Google Business Profile, directories, press and industry sites. Agents cross-check. Contradictions lose.

Actionable

A way to book, request or call that a machine can complete. If the only path is "fill out our 12-field form," the agent picks the competitor with a phone number.

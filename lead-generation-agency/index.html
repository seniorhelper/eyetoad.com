Three kinds of lead

Rented. Bought. Owned. Only one of them is an asset.

Every business owner has been sold "leads." Most have been burned by them. The difference isn't the vendor — it's the category.

Rented leads aren't evil — they're a fine way to fill a slow month. They're just not a business. A business is when the phone rings because someone typed "roofer near me," saw you first on the map, read a page that answered their question, and called. That's what we build.

How we build an owned source

Found. Chosen. Called.

A lead is a chain of three events, and most "lead generation" only touches the first one. We build all three.

Found — on all three surfaces

Google's ten results, the Google Maps pack, and AI answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, AI Mode). Each has its own rules. A business that wins one and ignores the others is invisible to most of its market. Local & maps · SEO services · AI search

Chosen — over the names next to yours

Reviews, proof, clear pricing signals, real photos, and pages that answer the exact question the customer typed. Being on the list gets you considered. Being the clearest option gets you picked.

Called — before they try the next one

A fast site, one obvious action, a form that actually works, and follow-up measured in minutes. Published research on lead response time shows the first hour decides who gets the customer. Why the first hour wins · Conversion optimization

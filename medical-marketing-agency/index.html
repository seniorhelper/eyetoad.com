What is different about medical

Patients search for a procedure. Then for a person.

"Dermatologist near me" is only the first search. The second is the condition. The third is the provider's name, the reviews, the photos, whether the practice takes their insurance, and increasingly a question to an AI assistant: "who should I see for this?" A practice that only wins the first search loses the patient at the third.

Procedure-level visibility

One page per procedure or condition you want to grow, written to answer the patient's actual question — cost, recovery, candidacy, what happens at the visit — and structured so Google and AI systems can cite it.

Providers as the entity

Patients choose a person. Provider pages with credentials, real photos, procedures and reviews that name them are what AI assistants and Google verify — and what turns a search into a booked appointment.

Compliance is a design constraint

No patient stories without consent, no outcome guarantees, no claims a regulator would flag, and forms and chat that stay HIPAA-conscious. Growth that creates a compliance problem isn't growth.

📌 Short answer

Industry-specific SEO means the strategy is built around how buyers in your category actually search — not a generic local-business template with your industry name swapped in. The keyword patterns, the point at which someone starts looking, the seasonal curve and the trust signals that close the sale are genuinely different by industry. Eye To Ad Media builds dedicated strategies for seventeen Denver verticals, and custom ones for anything else.

Most agencies run one playbook and change the nouns. It is efficient for the agency and it is why so many local campaigns produce rankings without producing calls.

The differences are not cosmetic. An emergency plumber and a med spa are not competing on the same surface, in the same season, with the same proof, or on the same timeline. Treating them the same way means at least one of them gets a strategy built for somebody else's business.

The question is never "how do we do SEO." It is "what does a buyer in this category do in the ninety seconds before they call somebody."

Four things that genuinely differ by industry

Not four things we say differ. Four things that change what you should actually build first.

1. Where the buyer is when they start searching

A burst pipe and a rhinoplasty are separated by about three weeks of consideration. The plumber's customer is on a phone, standing in water, choosing from three map listings in under a minute. The dermatology patient has read six articles, compared four practices and will check reviews again before they book. One needs proximity and a phone number. The other needs depth, credentials and independent corroboration. Building both the same way guarantees one of them is wrong.

2. When demand actually arrives

Denver has unusually sharp seasonal curves. Front Range hail compresses a year of roofing and auto glass demand into a few weeks. HVAC has two peaks rather than one. Gyms live or die in January, which means the ranking work happens the previous autumn. Legal and veterinary are close to flat. Getting this wrong is the most expensive scheduling mistake in local marketing, because rankings take months and seasons do not wait.

3. Which surface decides the sale

Some categories are won in the map pack and nowhere else. Some are won in long-form content that a buyer reads weeks before contacting anyone. Some are increasingly won inside an AI answer that never produces a click at all. The table below is the shortest honest version of that.

4. What a stranger needs to see before they trust you

Legal buyers look for case experience and bar standing. Aesthetic patients want before-and-after proof and provider qualifications. Home service buyers want licensing, local reviews and a real address. Restaurant diners want recent photos and recent reviews. The proof that converts in one category is noise in another, and occasionally actively counterproductive.

AI search does not affect every industry equally, and the split is about phrasing rather than category. Research on local AI Overviews found they appear on only around 15% of plain "near me" searches but roughly 77% of informational-local questions. When demand actually arrives in Denver

This is the part national advice cannot give you, because it is specific to the Front Range. Rankings take months to build and seasons do not wait, so the only workable rule is that the content has to exist before the season, not during it.

Jan – Feb

Resolution & recovery

Gym membership demand spikes hard and briefly. Cold-start and battery failures peak. Pothole and alignment damage follows the freeze-thaw cycle.

Fitness · Auto repair · Dentistry (insurance reset)

Mar – May

Thaw & first storms

Landscaping demand surges from the first warm week. Hail season opens, and roofing, collision and auto glass demand arrives in concentrated bursts.

Jun – Aug

Heat & mountain season

First real heat drives HVAC's first peak. I-70 weekends load brakes and transmissions. Aesthetic bookings run ahead of summer.

HVAC · Auto repair · Med spa · Restaurants

Sep – Nov

Prep season

Furnace demand at the first freeze. Tire changeovers and winter prep. And the quiet one — this is when January's gym rankings have to be built.

Dec

Deadline demand

Dental insurance benefits expire, producing a genuine end-of-year surge that most practices never market against.

Dentistry · Dermatology · Med spa

All year

Flat and continuous

Legal, veterinary and emissions repair have no meaningful season. Colorado registration renewals are spread evenly across every month, so failed-test demand never stops.

Law · Personal injury · Veterinary · Auto repair

The practical consequence

If your category has a season, the useful question is not "when should we start" but "how many months before the peak do we need to have finished". For roofing that means the work happens over winter. For gyms it means autumn. For HVAC it means twice a year, every year. Getting the calendar right is worth more than most of the tactics people argue about.

Every one of these strategies sits on top of the same foundation — technical health, a working conversion path, consistent business data. That general layer is described under Denver SEO, and the industry work goes on top of it. If the basics have never been done, start there.

The Three Pillars

Local SEO Rankings Are Built on Three Foundations

Google's local algorithm evaluates every business on three core signals. Miss any one of them and your competitors take the map pack position you should own.

📍

Proximity

Google measures how close your business is to the searcher. You can't change your address — but you can maximize your relevance for every searcher within your realistic service radius through smart service area targeting and location-specific landing pages.

🎯

Relevance

Does Google understand exactly what your business does and who it serves? Relevance is built through complete Google Business Profile categories, keyword-optimized descriptions, service listings, and consistent content that signals your specialization to Google's algorithm.

⭐

Prominence

How well-known and trusted is your business online? Prominence is built through reviews, citations, backlinks, and brand mentions across the web. The more authoritative signals Google finds about your business, the higher it ranks you in local results.

Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile Optimization: Your #1 Local Ranking Factor

Your Google Business Profile is the single most important element of your local SEO. It's what controls your appearance in Google Maps, the local pack, and Google's knowledge panel. Most Denver businesses set it up once and never touch it again — and then wonder why they're not ranking.

We treat your GBP as a living, active asset. That means ongoing optimization, regular posts, photo updates, Q&A management, and review response — all the signals Google uses to determine whether your business deserves the top spot.

🏷️

Category & Attribute Optimization

Choosing the right primary and secondary categories is one of the highest-impact local SEO moves. We research which categories your top competitors use and optimize accordingly.

📸

Photo & Video Strategy

Profiles with regular photo updates get significantly more views and direction requests. We build a photo strategy that signals active business presence to Google's algorithm.

⭐

Review Acquisition & Response

We build systematic review generation processes and manage responses — both positive and negative — in ways that strengthen trust signals and local ranking.

📝

Posts, Services & Q&A

Regular Google Posts, complete service listings, and proactive Q&A management keep your profile active and feed Google the relevance signals it needs to rank you higher.

What makes a law firm website different?

Law firm website design is the design and build of a site whose job is to turn a person in a legal situation into a booked consultation. It has to establish credibility in seconds, explain practice areas plainly, and make contact effortless, while meeting state bar advertising rules.

A person searching for a lawyer is often scared, angry or both. They are not browsing. They read the practice area page, check the attorney bio, look for reviews and decide in under a minute whether to call. The site has one job: make that decision easy.

We build hand-coded sites with no plugin bloat, starting at $99 a month with a buyout option, through our custom website design service. Firms that also want search visibility pair it with law firm SEO.

What is included in a law firm website build?

Strategy, design, copy, build, schema markup and launch, with a conversion review before it goes live. Our founder’s own sites hold multiple listings on one Google results page; see the case study for what the same methods produce.

Practice area pages that answer

What happens next, what it costs, how long it takes, what to bring. Answered plainly, in the client’s words.

Attorney bios that build trust

Bar admissions, years, real photographs, case types handled. Credentials stated accurately, never inflated.

Contact that works at night

Sticky call buttons, a short intake form and an optional AI chatbot that answers after hours without giving legal advice.

Fast and compliant

Fast pages, accessible markup and copy written to your state bar advertising rules, with every claim approved by you.

Questions, answered straight

How much does a law firm website cost?

Industry surveys put the average one-off agency project near $37,000. Our lease model starts at $99 a month with a buyout option at 24 to 36 months, and rates rise with size and complexity. We publish the reasoning on our custom website design page.

Do you follow bar advertising rules?

Yes. We write to the rules of your state bar, keep outcome claims conservative, and you approve every line before launch. We are not lawyers and will say so when a question needs one.

How long does a law firm site take?

A focused firm site usually launches in three to six weeks. Larger multi-practice sites take longer.

Will the site rank on Google?

A well-built site is the foundation. Ranking is separate work, covered by our law firm SEO service. No agency can guarantee rankings and we will not pretend otherwise.

Can you redesign our current site without losing rankings?

Yes, when the redesign keeps URLs, content and redirects intact. Read our guide on whether to rebuild or fix a website before deciding.

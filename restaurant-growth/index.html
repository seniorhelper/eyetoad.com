Questions owners ask

What is in the free restaurant analysis?

We review your website, Google Business Profile, reviews, menu pages and how you show up in Google, Maps and AI answers, then compare that with the restaurants competing for the same diners. You get a plain-English list of what to fix first. It costs nothing and there is no obligation.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. We earn the next month by delivering this month. The free analysis carries no obligation at all.

How do NFC and QR table menus work?

Guests tap their phone on a small table sticker or scan a QR code and your menu, specials or reviews page opens instantly. Menus are published as real web pages through linkrestaurants.com, so they can also be found in search.

What can AI agents do for a restaurant?

Practical jobs: answer after-hours questions about hours, menu and reservations, draft review replies for your approval, and follow up on catering and private event inquiries within minutes. You stay in control of what goes out.

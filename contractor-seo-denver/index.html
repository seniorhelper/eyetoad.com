What an AI Agent Actually Checks Before It Recommends a Contractor

None of this is exotic. It is the machine-readable version of what a careful homeowner would check — which is exactly why it is winnable, and why clean data beats a big budget here.

01

Does the business resolve to one entity?

Name, address and phone identical everywhere. Conflicting details across directories make you harder to trust and easier to skip.

02

Is the service area explicit?

An agent filtering for "Thornton" needs to see Thornton stated, not inferred from a Denver address. Unstated coverage is treated as no coverage.

03

Are the services machine-readable?

Service schema and dedicated pages per trade. A paragraph saying "we do it all" gives an agent nothing to match against.

04

Do reviews look current and real?

Recency and steady flow, not a pile from 2021. Response rate is a signal too — most businesses never reply, so replying stands out.

05

Are credentials verifiable?

Licence numbers, insurance, accreditations stated plainly and linked where possible. An unlinked claim is weaker than a linked one.

06

Is anything current?

Recent posts, updated hours, live profile activity. A dormant presence reads as a business that may not exist anymore.

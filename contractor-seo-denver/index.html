Seasonal Strategy

Why Seasonal SEO Is Critical for Denver Contractors

Denver's climate creates predictable demand spikes. The contractors that rank before the season starts capture the majority of those leads. The ones that start SEO after the spike are already too late.

⛈️

Spring Storm Season (April–June)

Denver hailstorms generate massive roofing and siding demand. The roofing companies that rank for "hail damage roof repair Denver" and "insurance claim roofing Denver" in March and April capture the surge. Build those rankings in February — not after the first storm hits.

☀️

Summer Construction (June–September)

Peak season for remodeling, landscaping, painting, and general contracting. Homeowners planning summer projects start searching in April and May. Content built in the winter compounds in time to capture this demand at peak volume.

🍂

Pre-Winter HVAC (September–November)

Furnace replacements, tune-ups, and emergency heating repairs spike as temperatures drop. HVAC companies that rank for "furnace repair Denver" before October don't scramble to buy ads when the cold hits — they're already at the top of the map pack.

❄️

Winter Emergency Services (December–March)

Frozen pipes, emergency heating failures, and ice dam roof damage create urgent, high-conversion searches. Emergency service pages with strong local SEO capture these high-intent leads year-round — not just in season.

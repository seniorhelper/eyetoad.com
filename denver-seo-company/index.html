The work itself

What a Denver SEO company actually does for a local business.

Strip away the jargon and the job has six parts. A company that skips any of them is selling a fraction of SEO as the whole thing.

1. Technical foundation

Speed, indexing, structured data and a site Google can read without guessing. On most Denver sites we audit this is where the quiet problems live: four-second mobile loads and service pages Google never found.

2. Google Business Profile and the map pack

Categories, services, attributes, geotagged photos, review velocity and replies, and a consistent name, address and phone everywhere. For a plumber in Lakewood or a dentist on Colfax the map pack is often the only result that matters.

3. Pages for the terms buyers use

Service and neighborhood pages for the exact phrases Denver customers search, written so a person would call from them. "Emergency furnace repair Highlands Ranch" needs its own page; a homepage paragraph will not rank for it.

4. Authority and mentions

Citations, directory consistency, press and partner mentions, and links from places that make sense for a Denver business, including properties we built ourselves.

5. AI search visibility

AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity now answer "who should I call" directly, citing businesses whose facts agree everywhere and whose pages state plain, quotable answers. We build that into every campaign instead of billing it separately.

6. Conversion

Click-to-call placement, a thirty-second form, a sub-two-second mobile load, an offer that answers the visitor's fear. Rankings that do not produce calls are an unfinished job.

Comparing your options

Denver SEO companies: how to compare them.

Search "best SEO company in Denver" and you get listicles ranking whoever paid for placement. A better method: put every company you are considering, including us, through one checklist and count the straight answers.

Ask every Denver SEO companyWhy it matters
Who, by name, will do the work on my account?Handoffs from sales to fulfilment are where campaigns quietly die. If the answer is a department, expect a template.
Can you show a business you own ranking on your own methods?A client screenshot can be cherry-picked. A company's own listings, checkable in Google right now, cannot.
What do you measure, and will I see calls, not impressions?Impressions rise on their own as Google shows more AI results. Calls and booked work pay the bills.
Is AI search part of the campaign or a separate line item?AI Overviews already sit above the map pack for many Denver service searches. Paying extra to be visible there is paying twice.
What happens to the site, the profile and the content if I leave?Some companies build on platforms you cannot export, so the rankings leave with them. Everything should be in your name.
Will you tell me when SEO is the wrong buy?A new business with no reviews and a two-month runway usually needs ads first. A company that never says no is selling, not advising.
What is the contract term and the exit?Twelve-month lock-ins exist to survive the months when nothing moves. Month to month keeps the company accountable.
How do you report, and how often do I talk to a person?A dashboard is not a conversation. You should be able to call the person doing the work.

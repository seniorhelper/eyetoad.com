    The seven areas

    Every campaign works these. Budget decides how deep.

    Think of it the way a builder thinks about a house. Every house needs a foundation, framing, plumbing, electrical, a roof, finish work and an inspection. A bigger budget doesn't invent a new category — it builds a bigger house, faster, with better materials.

    01

    Technical foundation

    Speed, crawlability, structured data, indexing hygiene, the things a search engine has to trust before it will trust anything else on the site. Examples of the kind of work: Core Web Vitals, schema, internal architecture.

    02

    Content that answers

    Pages built around what customers actually type and ask — service pages, city pages, guides — written by people who have sold the thing, not summarized it. Examples: money pages, question-shaped sections, direct-answer capsules.

    03

    Local & maps

    The map pack is the fastest lever a local business has. Examples: Google Business Profile depth, review systems, citations, service-area signals.

    04

    Authority & mentions

    Being referenced by other places that matter — including properties we own. Examples: press distribution, directory presence, vertical networks, editorial mentions.

    05

    AI visibility

    AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT and Perplexity cite sources that are clear, verifiable and machine-readable. Examples: entity signals, fact blocks, llms.txt, citation-eligible structure. How GEO works.

    06

    Conversion

    A site that ranks and doesn't ring is a waste of everyone's time. Examples: offer clarity, calls-to-action, forms that work, speed-to-lead, sales-trained chatbots. Conversion optimization.

    07

    Reporting you can read

    Calls, forms, map views, rankings that matter — in plain English, tied to revenue, not a 40-page PDF of vanity metrics. And when the strategy changes, you hear why.

    What changed

    Your customers search in three places now.

    Google. Google Maps. And AI — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google's own AI Overviews and AI Mode. A business that only wins one of the three is invisible to the other two. Most agencies still optimize for one.

    Found. Chosen. Called. You need all three.

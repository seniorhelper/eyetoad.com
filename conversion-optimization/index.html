Common Problems

Where Denver Business Websites Lose Leads Every Day

Most of these problems are invisible to the business owner. Visitors leave without calling and you never know why. Here's what we find most often.

📱

Mobile Experience Failures

Over 60% of local searches happen on mobile. Phone numbers that aren't tap-to-call, buttons too small to tap, text that requires zooming, and layouts that shift on mobile all kill conversions from your highest-intent traffic source.

Most common issue
🐌

Slow Page Load Times

Google's own research shows 53% of mobile users abandon a page that takes more than 3 seconds to load. Every second of delay reduces conversions measurably. Uncompressed images, bloated scripts, and poor hosting are silent lead killers.

High impact, fast fix
🔇

Weak or Missing CTAs

If a visitor doesn't immediately see what action to take, they leave. CTAs buried below the fold, generic "Contact Us" buttons with no urgency, and pages with no visible phone number are leaving leads on the table every day.

Fixable in hours
🤔

Unclear Value Proposition

Visitors decide within seconds whether your business can solve their problem. If your homepage doesn't immediately communicate what you do, who you serve, and why you're better than the alternatives — they leave for a competitor who does.

Messaging issue
😰

Missing Trust Signals

People don't contact businesses they don't trust. Missing reviews, no certifications displayed, no real photos of your team or work, and no local address visible are all red flags to potential customers evaluating whether to reach out.

Trust issue
📋

Friction-Heavy Forms

Long contact forms with too many required fields, forms that don't work on mobile, or forms with no confirmation message destroy conversion rates. The easier it is to reach out, the more people will. Every extra field reduces submissions.

Quick win

Our Process

How We Build Your Conversion Optimization System

Conversion optimization isn't guesswork — it's a structured audit, prioritization, and implementation process. Here's exactly what we do.

1

Conversion Audit

We audit your site's conversion path from the perspective of a buyer in your target market — identifying every point of friction, missing trust signal, and lost opportunity on your highest-traffic pages.

2

Priority Ranking by Impact

Not all fixes are equal. We rank every issue by its likely impact on lead volume and implementation difficulty — so the highest-value changes happen first and results start moving fast.

3

Implementation

We implement the changes — whether that's technical speed improvements, CTA rewrites, trust signal placement, form simplification, or mobile layout fixes. Changes are tracked so we can measure impact.

4

Measurement & Iteration

We track conversion rate changes, call volume, and form submissions before and after each change. What's working gets reinforced. What isn't gets adjusted. The system improves continuously.

5

Integration with SEO

Conversion improvements feed back into SEO — better engagement signals, lower bounce rates, and improved page experience scores all support local and organic rankings simultaneously.

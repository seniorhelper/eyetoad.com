Why do painters lose jobs they should win?

SEO for painting contractors is the work of getting a painting company found when homeowners and property managers search for interior, exterior, cabinet or commercial painting in its service area, in Google results, the Maps pack and AI answers, then turning those searches into estimates.

Painting is bought on trust and timing. The homeowner searches in March, reads three reviews, calls the first company that looks real and books the spring slot. The best painter in town never got the call because the search ended before it reached them.

We run search for painting companies in our own market and beyond, and our founder managed sales teams for national remodeling companies. The full trades playbook lives at Advertising For Contractors.

What does painting contractor SEO include?

Google Business Profile work, job-type pages, review generation, local SEO for every town you paint in, and AI-answer visibility. Sister pages: contractor marketing, roofing SEO and plumbing SEO.

Season first

Exterior demand peaks in spring and early summer. Rankings take months, so the campaign starts in winter. Our contractor page has a season planner.

One page per job type

Interior, exterior, cabinets, commercial, HOA. Each is its own search and its own page.

Reviews that name the street

A review that says “Wash Park bungalow, two coats” ranks the neighborhood. We build the ask into your close-out.

Photos that convert

Before-and-after sets on the pages that rank. Painters win on visible proof more than almost any trade.

Questions, answered straight

How much does SEO cost for a painting company?

Most campaigns run $500 to $2,500 a month depending on service area and competition, with a $100 entry option. Published surveys put the average agency retainer above $3,000.

When should a painter start SEO?

Winter. Rankings built in January are earning in April. Starting in May means competing for a season that is already booked.

Do lead platforms work for painters?

They can fill gaps, but you pay for shared leads whether you win or not. Search is the asset you own.

Do you work with painters outside Colorado?

Yes. Every market gets its own plan because the season, the housing stock and the competitors differ.

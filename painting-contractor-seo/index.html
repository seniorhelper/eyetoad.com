What is different about painting

Seasonal, service-specific, and sold to four crews at once.

"Exterior painters near me" in April and "cabinet painting" in November are different customers with different questions. Most painter sites have one services page and a gallery — and buy the rest from a platform that also sold that lead to three competitors. The way out is a page per service, ranking before its season, and an estimate path fast enough to win the comparison.

A page per service, ready before the season

Exterior, interior, cabinets, decks and fences, commercial, HOA and multi-family. Each with its own page answering the homeowner's real questions — prep, product, timing, warranty, what happens on the visit — published before the searches spike.

The estimate is the conversion

A homeowner comparing three quotes picks the fastest, clearest one. A phone-first estimate path, honest pricing signals and a sales-trained assistant that books the walkthrough beat a twelve-field form.

Proof a machine can verify

Real before/after photos, reviews that name the crew and the neighborhood, licensing and insurance stated plainly, and facts that agree across the site, the Business Profile and the directories — what AI assistants check before recommending a painter.

How we do it

Ranking by spring. Booked by May. Owned by fall.

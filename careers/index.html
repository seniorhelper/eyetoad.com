Who are we looking for?

Eye To Ad Media is looking mainly for sales representatives who enjoy talking with small business owners, and also for developers, designers, writers, video editors and SEO specialists. Experience helps. Honesty, follow-through and curiosity matter more.

Sales representatives

You talk with owners of local businesses, find out what is broken in their marketing and show them what fixing it looks like. No scripts that make you cringe. If you have sold home services, advertising, software or cars, you already have the muscles.

Builders and makers

Front-end developers, 3D and WebGL tinkerers, designers, copywriters and video editors. Look at our work. If you think you could make it better, we want to meet you.

Search specialists

Local SEO, technical SEO, AI search visibility and conversion work. You should care more about a client’s phone ringing than about a ranking report.

Questions before you apply

What positions is Eye To Ad Media hiring for?

Mainly sales representatives who can talk with local business owners about search marketing. We also want to hear from web developers, designers, writers, video editors and SEO specialists. If you are good at something a marketing company needs, apply.

Is this remote?

Most of our work is done remotely, and sales conversations happen by phone, video and in person depending on the client. Our office is at 1001 Bannock Street in Denver.

Do I need SEO experience to sell for you?

No. You need to be honest, curious and comfortable on the phone. We teach the product. Our founder spent years managing sales teams for multi-million-dollar home-service companies and trains reps personally.

How does pay work?

Compensation depends on the role and is discussed in the first conversation. We do not publish figures on this page, and we will not waste your time if the fit is not there.

What happens after I apply?

A person reads every application. If there may be a fit, you will hear from us within a few business days to set up a short call. If you do not hear back in a week, call 1-800-481-8638 and ask.

Anatomy of a brand that sells

Six things we look at before we spend a dollar on ads.

A strong brand makes every other channel cheaper. These are the six we audit first, and they are free to check yourself.

01

A promise in one sentence

If you cannot say what you do and why you are the better choice in one breath, neither can your customer. We write that sentence first.

02

A look people remember

Color, type and logo that are ownable, not borrowed from the category. The test: would a customer recognize you with the name covered?

03

A voice that sounds like you

Plain, confident and specific beats clever and vague. The same voice on the site, the truck, the ads and the voicemail.

04

Proof in the right places

Reviews, photos of real work, credentials and guarantees placed exactly where doubt shows up, next to the price and the button.

05

Consistency everywhere

The same name, promise and look on Google, the site, social and print. Consistency is how people and AI tools decide you are one real business.

06

One idea to own

Fastest, friendliest, most transparent, the one who answers at 2 a.m. Pick the idea competitors are not claiming, then prove it every day.

Straight answers

Questions owners ask us

What is the Ad Lab?

The Ad Lab is Eye To Ad Media's creative think tank: the part of our team that studies what your competitors are doing, finds the angle they are missing, and turns it into a brand, website and ads built to convert. It works hand in hand with our SEO and AI search work, so the traffic we earn lands on something worth clicking.

Is this branding, web design or advertising?

All three, pointed at one number: revenue. We look at your brand, your website's conversion path and your ad creative together, because a great ad sending people to a confusing page wastes the budget either way.

How do you measure whether creative is working?

With calls, form fills, booked jobs and click-through rates, not opinions. We test new headlines, offers and layouts against the current version and keep whichever wins.

Do you test ideas before using them on my business?

Yes. We run our own companies on the same playbook, including local search, landing pages and ad creative, and only bring tactics to clients once they have worked for us.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, and we quote after a short call. For context, an Ahrefs survey of 439 providers put the average agency retainer at $3,209 a month. Most of our campaigns run between $500 and $3,000 a month, with a $100 entry option, and some clients get bonus advertising at no charge.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest agency can guarantee rankings or revenue, and Google says so in its own guidance on hiring an SEO. What we guarantee is honest work, clear reporting on calls and conversions, and telling you plainly what is and is not working.

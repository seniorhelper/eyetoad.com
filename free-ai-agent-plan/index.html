Size tiers and what the market charges

Each recommended agent gets a size. S is one job with one or two connections, M means several tools or sensitive data, L means a system of cooperating agents for a larger team. Agencies that publish prices put those builds at roughly $5k–$25k, $15k–$75k and $100k–$450k+ (Neoteric, Cleveroad, DP Vision). You won't see our price here because we haven't seen your workflow yet. After the free 30-minute call you get one fixed number, and small-business single-job builds usually sit near the bottom of those bands.

The readiness score

It starts at 40 out of 100. Points are added for a CRM, a business email account, a spreadsheet or accounting system, an online calendar, a few connected tools and written approval rules. Points come off when records live on paper. The three gaps worth the most points become your "fix first" list, and most take an afternoon, not a project.

Why no DIY prompts

A plan you can act on, not a prompt to paste.

You won't find a block of text to copy into a chatbot anywhere in your plan, and that's deliberate. A prompt you paste into a chat window can't open your inbox at 2 a.m., book the job or update your books — a coded agent connected to your tools can.

The value lives in everything around the AI: secure access to your accounts with the narrowest permissions, the approval step before anything risky, a log of every action, a switch that pauses it instantly, and weeks of testing on your real messages until it gets them right. That is the work we do. Your plan's job is to show what's worth doing and in what order.

Questions

Free AI agent plan: quick answers.

Is the AI agent plan really free?

Yes. There is no signup to see it, no card and no catch. Your plan appears on this page as soon as you finish step five, and the 30-minute review call to talk it through is free too.

Which tasks should I automate with AI first?

Start with work that is frequent, repetitive and low-risk if a draft comes out wrong: sorting email, answering new leads quickly, reminders and receipts. Keep anything that needs your judgment, like setting prices or a hard conversation, with a person. The planner ranks your chores that way.

How accurate are the hours and dollar estimates?

They are planning numbers built from your own answers and a cautious 50 to 70 percent share per chore, not a guarantee. On the review call we check them against a real week and adjust them before anything is quoted.

Does the plan include prompts or setup steps I can use myself?

No. The plan describes what each agent would do, which of your tools it would work with and what you would approve. Building, connecting, testing and running the agent is the work we do, and you own the finished result.

What is an AI readiness assessment for a small business?

It is a quick check of whether your tools and habits are ready for an agent: where customer records live, whether email runs on a business account and whether approval rules are written down. Your plan includes a readiness score from 0 to 100 and the three gaps to close first.

What happens to my answers?

They stay in your browser until you decide to send them. If you email yourself the plan or book a review, we use what you send only to reply to you, and we never sell your information.

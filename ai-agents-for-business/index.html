Plain English

What is an AI agent? Think "new hire who never sleeps."

Picture the most reliable assistant you have ever had: you explain the job once, hand over the right keys, and it gets done every day, at 2 a.m. if need be. An agent reads what comes in, picks the next step from rules you agreed to, uses your software to do it, and logs everything it touched.

Here is the honest line between the three things people usually mean. The short version from TechTarget: chatbots converse and answer from scripts, while agents act across systems to complete tasks.

Safe by design

Your agent works for you, inside rules you set.

An agent with keys to your inbox deserves the care you would give a new hire with keys to the shop. House rules on every build:

Human approval rules

Refunds, quotes, anything sent to a new person: we list what needs your yes, and the agent is coded so it cannot skip that step.

Least-privilege access

It gets only the permissions its job needs. A reporting agent can read your sales; it cannot issue a refund.

A full audit log

Every email read, row written and message sent is logged with a timestamp.

Your data stays yours

We use business-tier AI services whose terms say your data is not used to train their models, and we never use it to train anything ourselves.

A kill switch

One switch pauses it instantly. Nothing breaks; work waits for a person.

Compliance notes

Health businesses: no patient health information goes through an agent without a signed BAA and a HIPAA-eligible setup. Finance: money movement always needs a human approval.

You own the code and prompts

The code, the prompts and the configuration are yours. Leave us someday and the agent goes with you.

What we won't automate

Hiring and firing, apologies to a loyal customer, anything legally binding. Some moments need you.

How we price

Every Eye To Ad Media agent is quoted as a fixed price after a free 30-minute workflow audit — no hourly meter. Most single-task agents for small businesses land at the low end of these ranges. If the hours you would get back do not clearly pay for the build, we will tell you on the call and suggest something cheaper, even if that is a spreadsheet formula.

The running costs, separate from the build

  • Hosting: a lightweight agent typically runs $50–$300 a month on its server.
  • Model usage: the AI service bills per task processed. A few hundred emails a month costs a lot less than a few thousand; we estimate yours in the blueprint and set a hard monthly cap.
  • Care: industry maintenance runs 15–30% of build cost a year — monitoring, fixes when your tools update, and tuning.

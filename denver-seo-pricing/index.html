The Honest Part

What Actually Influences Your Pricing, Timeline & ROI

Anyone who quotes SEO without asking about these is guessing. These factors shape both what your campaign costs and how fast it pays off.

🌱

Domain Age & Authority

Established domains carry trust that ranks faster. Brand-new domains take longer to gain traction — though creative strategy can shorten that curve dramatically.

🥊

Competition Level

Crowded industries like law, home services, and healthcare demand more content, links, and authority than low-competition niches.

🩺

Current Site Health

A fast, technically clean site ranks sooner. Sites with technical debt need foundational work first — which a free audit uncovers.

📍

Geographic Targeting

Ranking in one neighborhood is faster and cheaper than dominating the whole Denver metro or going statewide.

⚡

Growth Pace

Aggressive timelines need more resources up front. A steady starter pace costs less but covers ground more gradually.

💵

Your Close Rate & Customer Value

The same lead flow produces very different ROI depending on how well you close and what a customer is worth to you.

Here's our edge: we've built a knack for getting creative and getting our clients up next to the big dogs and industry leaders — even from a standing start. We'll always tell you honestly what your situation calls for.

The short answer

What Are Denver SEO Services?

Definition

Denver SEO services are the ongoing work of making a business findable by people searching in the Denver market. In practice that is six workstreams running at once: technical SEO so search engines can crawl and index the site, on-page optimization so each page targets one clear intent, content built around what buyers actually search, local SEO and Google Business Profile work to compete in the map pack, link authority to make the domain competitive enough to rank at all, and reporting that ties the work to leads rather than impressions. Since 2025 a seventh has become unavoidable: optimizing for AI Overviews and AI assistants, which increasingly answer the question before anyone clicks a result.

That list is roughly the same at every competent agency. What differs is the order of operations, how much of it gets done rather than reported on, and whether anyone connects the work to revenue at the end of the month.

Denver makes the difference sharper than most markets. It is a metro of distinct submarkets rather than one city — a campaign built to rank in Denver proper will not put you in the map pack in Thornton, and one built for Castle Rock does not transfer to Aurora. Meanwhile the categories with the highest ticket values here, legal, healthcare and real estate, have been fought over by well-funded agencies for a decade. Published Denver and Colorado pricing guides in 2026 consistently place those categories in the $3,000–$5,000 a month range for that reason, and national survey data puts the average agency retainer near $3,200.

What we do differently

Three things, and only one of them is unusual.

The owner scopes every campaign. Zach Wennstedt has run this company since 2012 and still writes the strategy on every account rather than handing it to a junior manager after the sale. That is not a boast about size; it is the reason the plan matches the business.

We optimize for AI answers as part of the core work, not as an upsell. When someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview for a recommendation, two or three businesses get named. Fourth place is the same as invisible. That work — entity consistency across the web, content structured to be extractable, schema markup, review signals — is included in every campaign here. Other Denver agencies do this too now, and the ones that do are the ones you should be comparing us against.

We publish what it costs and who it does not suit. Both are further down this page. Neither is a competitive advantage so much as an admission that the alternative — "it depends, book a call" — wastes everyone's afternoon.

Every Denver SEO Campaign Includes All Six Pillars

Not a menu you assemble from. Rankings come from these working together, so they run together — the mix shifts by business, the coverage does not.

Technical SEO

The foundation. If search engines cannot crawl, render and index your site cleanly, nothing else you do matters.

  • Crawlability and indexation auditing
  • Core Web Vitals and page speed
  • Schema markup and structured data
  • Canonicalization and duplicate content
  • Mobile usability and rendering

On-Page Optimization

One page, one intent. Most Denver sites lose rankings because three pages compete for the same term and none of them wins.

  • Keyword-to-page mapping
  • Title, meta and heading structure
  • Internal linking architecture
  • Content depth and topical coverage
  • Image optimization and alt text
✍️

Content Strategy

Content built around what buyers search before they buy — not around a monthly word count.

  • Buyer-intent keyword research
  • Service and city page development
  • Supporting articles that link inward
  • FAQ content with matching schema
  • Refreshing pages that have gone stale

Local SEO & Google Maps

For most Denver service businesses the map pack outperforms the organic results below it. It is also the fastest thing to move.

The number that actually decides your budget

Not our tier names. Yours. Take your average ticket, multiply by your gross margin, multiply by your close rate on inbound leads. That is the most you can pay for a lead and still make money.

average ticket × gross margin × close rate = your maximum cost per lead

A Denver contractor with a $12,000 average job, 35% margin and a 25% close rate can afford roughly $1,050 per lead before the math stops working. At that number, a $3,000 monthly campaign needs three leads to break even. A dentist with a $400 first visit and a $2,800 lifetime value is solving a completely different equation, and should be quoted differently.

Straight talk

When You Should Not Hire a Denver SEO Agency

Including us. These are the situations where we turn work away, and telling you now is cheaper for both of us than telling you in month four.

You need customers this month

SEO is the wrong instrument. Map pack movement takes 30–90 days and organic takes longer. If the calendar is empty in three weeks, run Google Ads and Local Services Ads now, and start SEO alongside it so you are not renting traffic forever.

Your margin is broken

Traffic multiplies whatever the transaction already does. If you lose money or barely break even on each job, more leads accelerate the problem. Fix pricing or cost structure first — that is a better use of the next $3,000 than any campaign we could sell you.

Nobody searches for what you sell

SEO captures existing demand; it does not create it. Genuinely new categories need demand generation — content, social, PR, paid — before search volume exists to compete for. We will tell you if the volume is not there.

You cannot answer the phone

Leads leak faster than they arrive. Speed to lead is the single biggest determinant of close rate for service businesses, and no amount of ranking fixes a phone that rings out. Sort out answering before you pay to make it ring more.

You cannot commit past six months

Stopping at month four is the worst possible outcome. You pay for the entire foundation phase and quit immediately before it compounds. If six months of budget is not realistic, spend it on paid instead — you will get more from it.

You want a guarantee of #1

Nobody controls Google's rankings, and anyone promising a position is selling something else. We will forecast, we will commit to the work and the reporting, and we will show you the math. We will not promise a rank.

If none of those describe you, the rest of this page is the offer. If one of them does, call anyway — telling you what to do instead takes ten minutes and costs nothing.

Why pixels, not characters. Google renders titles in roughly 20px Arial and cuts at about 600 pixels on desktop, near 500 on mobile. Descriptions run to roughly 920 and 680. This measures your actual text in that font, so a title full of capitals gets flagged and a narrow one is allowed to run longer.

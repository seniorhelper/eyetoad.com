Google Cuts Titles at 600 Pixels. Not 60 Characters.

Which is why yours looks fine in your CMS and gets chopped in the results. A capital W is nearly four times the width of a lowercase i, so counting characters tells you almost nothing. Type your title in below and watch where it actually gets cut.

Direct answer: These free SEO tools need no signup and run entirely in your browser: a SERP preview that measures titles in pixels the way Google cuts them, a title tag generator, a schema markup generator and a lead cost calculator. Why pixels, not characters. Google renders titles in roughly 20px Arial and cuts at about 600 pixels on desktop, near 500 on mobile. Descriptions run to roughly 920 and 680. This measures your actual text in that font, so a title full of capitals gets flagged and a narrow one is allowed to run longer.

Two rules that matter more than the markup. One: everything in here
has to match your Google Business Profile and your website exactly — same name, same
punctuation, same suite number. Contradictory records are why search engines get uncertain
about you. Two: never mark up a rating you cannot show. Self-declared review scores are
against Google's guidelines and can cost you rich results entirely.

Tool 6

FAQ Schema Generator


Question-shaped sections are the most extractable content on a page — for Google's rich results and for AI answers. Paste your visible questions and answers; get the markup.




Turn a visible FAQ into FAQPage markup
Live
Your FAQPage JSON-LD
The one rule Google enforces. Every question in the markup must be visible on the page, word for word. Markup that says more than the page does is a violation, not a shortcut. Getting the questions right — the ones customers actually ask — is the part worth paying for.











Tool 7

Article Schema Generator


Blog posts with Article markup, a named author and real dates are what AI systems and Google's news surfaces can attribute. Anonymous, undated posts are the ones that never get cited.

The author is the point. A real person with a real page is a trust signal machines can verify. The markup just tells them where to look. If your posts have no author, adding one is worth more than the schema.

Tool 9

robots.txt Generator


Most robots.txt files are copied from a template written before AI search existed. This one welcomes the crawlers that decide whether you get cited, and keeps the one folder you should protect out of the index.




A robots.txt that does not hide you from AI

Your robots.txt
Blocking AI crawlers is opting out of AI answers. If you sell to the public, you almost certainly want them in. If you are not sure what a rule does, do not add it — a bad robots.txt is the fastest way to disappear from Google, and it is the first thing we check in an audit.











Tool 10

Business Info Consistency Checker


AI systems and Google cross-check your name, address and phone across your site, your Business Profile and the directories. One mismatch and you fail verification. Paste the three versions and see the differences.

Format differences are fine; fact differences are not. "St" versus "Street" is normalized. A different suite number, a different phone, a different business name — those are the ones that cost you. Fixing them across every directory is tedious, unglamorous work. It is also the kind we do first.

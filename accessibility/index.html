Our Commitment to Accessibility

Eye To Ad Media ("we," "us," or "our") is dedicated to ensuring that our website, www.eyetoad.com, is accessible to all individuals, including those with disabilities. We believe that every person deserves equal access to information and digital services, and we are actively working to meet and exceed applicable accessibility standards.

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, as published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including users who rely on screen readers, keyboard navigation, voice control, or other assistive technologies.

Accessibility Standards We Follow

Our accessibility efforts are guided by the following standards and legal frameworks:

📋

WCAG 2.1 AA

Web Content Accessibility Guidelines published by the W3C — the internationally recognized standard for digital accessibility.

🏛️

Accessibility Features on This Website

We have implemented the following features and design practices to improve accessibility:

  • Keyboard accessibility: All interactive elements — navigation links, buttons, and forms — are fully operable via keyboard (Tab, Enter, Space, arrow keys).
  • Screen reader support: Pages use semantic HTML5 elements, ARIA landmark roles, and descriptive aria-label attributes to provide a logical reading experience.
  • Focus indicators: Visible focus outlines are applied to all interactive elements for users navigating by keyboard.
  • Text resizing: Our layout adapts when browser text size is increased up to 200% without loss of content or functionality.
  • Color independence: Information is never conveyed by color alone; additional text, icons, or patterns are used.
  • Contrast ratios: Text-to-background contrast meets or exceeds WCAG 2.1 AA requirements.
  • Descriptive link text: Link text clearly describes the destination or action, avoiding generic phrases like "click here."
  • Page titles: Every page has a unique, descriptive <title> element.
  • Language attribute: The lang="en" attribute is set on every page to assist screen readers with pronunciation.
  • No auto-playing media: We do not use auto-playing video or audio that could disrupt screen reader users.

Known Limitations

While we strive for full WCAG 2.1 AA conformance, we acknowledge that some areas of our website may not yet fully meet all accessibility guidelines. Known or potential limitations include:

  • Third-party tools: Some embedded tools, widgets, or plugins (such as analytics dashboards or chat tools) are provided by third-party vendors and may not fully conform to WCAG 2.1 AA. We work with vendors to encourage accessibility improvements.
  • Legacy content: Older blog posts or documents may contain elements that predate our current accessibility standards. We prioritize updating high-traffic pages first.
  • PDF documents: Some downloadable documents may not be fully accessible. Please contact us if you need an accessible version of any document.

We are actively working to remediate these issues and appreciate your patience as we continue improving.

Assistive Technology Compatibility

This website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies and user agents:

  • Screen readers: JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver (macOS/iOS), TalkBack (Android)
  • Browsers: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (current and prior major version)
  • Voice control software: Dragon NaturallySpeaking, Voice Control (macOS/iOS)
  • Screen magnification tools: ZoomText, Windows Magnifier, browser zoom up to 200%
  • Switch access and alternative input devices

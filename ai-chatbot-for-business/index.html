What we actually build

Not a Pop-Up That Says “How Can I Help You?”

Most website chat is a greeting attached to a form. Ours is a knowledge base with a conversation on the front of it — it knows your services, your pricing logic, your service area and your objections.

Trained on your business

Your services, your ranges, the questions you answer on the phone forty times a week, and the ones you wish people asked. Not a generic script with your logo on it.

Qualifies, not just captures

It asks what they need, where they are and how soon — so the lead that reaches you is already scoped. Lead qualification time drops sharply when this happens before you pick up the phone.

Handles the objection

Price, timing, whether you cover their area, whether you are any good. The four things that stop a visitor calling get answered in the moment rather than never.

Honest about what it is

It says it is a bot when asked, and hands off to a person when that is the better answer. Pretending otherwise gets found out around message three and costs you the lead.

Runs on your site, not a platform

One script file on your own domain. No per-seat subscription to a third party, no data leaving to a service you have never heard of, nothing that stops working when a vendor changes their plans.

Knows which page it is on

A visitor on a pricing page and one on a service page get different openings. Context is most of what makes a conversation feel like one.

Before you buy one from anyone

What a Chatbot Will Not Fix

Every vendor in this category will tell you it solves everything. Here is what it does not.

It will not rescue a site nobody visits

A chatbot converts traffic you already have. If the problem is that nobody finds you, this is the wrong purchase and search work or local visibility is the right one. We will say so.

It will not replace answering the phone

Most local buyers still want a human, and a bot that blocks that path converts worse, not better. Ours puts your number in front of people early and often.

It will not close for you

It gets you a warm, scoped lead at two in the morning. What happens on the follow-up call is still your sales process — and that is where most of the money is won or lost.

It will not fix a slow website

If your pages take five seconds on a phone, most visitors are gone before they ever see a chat bubble. Speed comes first, always.

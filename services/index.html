What We Do Not Do

A list of services with no edges is a list you cannot trust. Here are ours.

  • We do not guarantee rankings. Nobody controls the algorithm. Anyone promising page one by a date is selling you something.
  • We do not buy links or reviews. Both are detectable and both can cost you the asset you spent years building.
  • We do not take every client. We do not work on adult, cannabis, alcohol, firearms or anything we would call derogatory.
  • We do not hand you to a junior. The founder works on accounts directly. That is also why the roster stays small.
  • We do not own your accounts. Your Business Profile and ad accounts are yours, during and after. If an agency will not hand those over, that is the answer to your question about them.

And we are not full service. We are a search and conversion shop. If what you need is programmatic display, radio, television, direct mail or a broader growth system, that lives with our sister company Search Converts — same team, wider remit.
